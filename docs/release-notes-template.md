## Voice It {VERSION} (beta)

First public release. Hold a key, speak, and cleaned-up text is pasted into the app you're using. It runs entirely on your Mac.

- Permissions may need re-approving after each update
- In password fields, and while Terminal's "Secure Keyboard Entry" is on, Voice It does nothing, by design
- It's a beta from a hobby project. Not recommended for confidential or regulated material
